Privacy Policy

Applies to the app Liri Media Player (Android, Android TV, Fire TV). Last updated: 26 October 2026

In short

There is no account with the developer. The app contains no ads, no tracking and no usage analytics. The developer only receives data from you if you choose to send a crash or problem report.

Controller

Developer of Liri Media Player, Switzerland. Contact: mediaplayerliri@proton.me

Data on your device

Stored on your device: login details and playlists, history (recently watched channels, playback progress), watchlist, My List and settings (including the parental control PIN). This data does not leave the device unless you create a backup file yourself.

Connections made by the app

Servers you enter: The app loads playlists, programme guide (EPG), images and streams directly from the servers you enter. These servers see your IP address and your login details. Their handling of data is the responsibility of the respective provider.

TMDB (USA): Posters, descriptions and other movie and series information come from The Movie Database (TMDB), USA. Your IP address and the TMDB number of the title are sent to TMDB (no login details, no search terms). The TMDB privacy policy applies.

Reports: only if you send a crash or problem report (see below).

There are no other connections.

Crash and problem reports

After a crash the app shows the report and asks whether to send it to the developer; under Settings → About you can also “Report a problem”. A report is only sent if you choose “Send”, or if you have turned on “Send crash reports automatically” (off by default, can be turned off at any time).

Content: error message, app version, device model, Android version, app language, your optional note and, for “Report a problem”, a technical log of the app. Server addresses, usernames and passwords are removed before sending.

Delivery: the report is passed through a Cloudflare Worker (Cloudflare, USA) and sent by e-mail via Resend (USA) to the developer. Your IP address is technically visible to Cloudflare but is not forwarded or stored by us. Reports are kept only as long as needed to fix the problem. Legal basis: your consent; you can ask us by e-mail to delete a report.

Backup files

Backup files (Settings → Backup) contain login details and the PIN. They are saved where you choose. Keep them safe.

Children

The app does not collect data from children. Parental control lets you lock categories with a PIN.

Deletion and your rights

Delete the history under Settings → History, and all data by uninstalling the app or using “Clear data” in the device settings. Apart from reports you have sent, the developer stores no data about you. Questions are welcome by e-mail.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published here.
